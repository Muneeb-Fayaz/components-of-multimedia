Multimedia basics

What is Multimedia?

Multimedia combines different forms of media to communicate information.

  • It can include text, pictures, sound, video and animation.
  • It makes learning and communication more engaging.
  • It is used in education, entertainment, business and websites.
01TextWords and characters 02ImagesPhotos, drawings, graphics 03AudioSpeech, music, sound effects 04VideoMoving real-world visuals 05AnimationCreated movement and effects

Together, these elements create a richer multimedia experience. Each section below has a small demo you can try.

Component 01

Text

The written information in a multimedia project.

  • Includes titles, headings, labels and paragraphs.
  • Helps explain ideas clearly and accurately.
  • Examples: captions, menus, instructions and subtitles.

EXAMPLE A title and captions in an educational video

Try it: layers of text on a video frame

Chapter 2 · Photosynthesis How Plants Make Food Plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide.

Turn the layers off and the frame still looks nice, but it no longer explains anything. Text carries the exact meaning.

Component 02

Images & Graphics

Still visuals that help people understand information.

  • Includes photographs, illustrations, icons, charts and diagrams.
  • Makes content attractive and easier to remember.
  • Common formats include JPEG, PNG and SVG.
JPEGPNGSVG

EXAMPLE A diagram showing parts of a computer

Try it: parts of a computer (this diagram is an SVG)

Pick a part. A diagram explains in one look what a paragraph would need many lines to say.

Component 03

Audio

Sound that adds meaning and emotion.

  • Includes speech, narration, music and sound effects.
  • Can guide attention and make a presentation lively.
  • Common formats include MP3, WAV and AAC.
MP3WAVAAC

EXAMPLE Voice-over explaining a science concept

Try it: sound waves (turn your volume up a little)

Sound is a wave. Changing its shape changes how it feels to the ear, from soft (sine) to buzzy (square).

Component 04

Video

A sequence of moving images, often with sound.

  • Shows real events, demonstrations and stories.
  • Useful for tutorials, news, films and online classes.
  • Common formats include MP4, MOV and WebM.
MP4MOVWebM

EXAMPLE A short video demonstrating an experiment

Try it: frame → motion

A video is a row of still frames shown quickly. Slow the speed down and you can see the single frames the motion is made from.

Component 05

Animation

The illusion of movement created from images or objects.

  • Can be 2D, 3D or motion graphics.
  • Explains processes that are difficult to film in real life.
  • Used in cartoons, simulations, games and advertisements.
2D3DMotion

EXAMPLE An animated model of the solar system

Try it: a simple solar system model

MercuryVenusEarthMars

Filming real planets is impossible, so animation shows the idea instead. Inner planets circle faster than outer ones.

Where is Multimedia Used?

Education

Online classes, tutorials and e-learning

Entertainment

Films, music, animation and video games

Business

Presentations, product demos and advertising

Healthcare

Medical training and visual explanations

Web & Apps

Interactive websites and mobile applications

Benefits of Multimedia

Clearer communication

Combines words, visuals and sound.

Better engagement

Keeps audiences interested.

Easy learning

Makes complex ideas easier to understand.

More memorable

Visual and audio cues support recall.

Flexible delivery

Works across classrooms, websites and apps.

Interactive potential

Can let users explore and respond.
